Unicode macros (layer 3)

Typed via Ctrl+Shift+U + hex + Enter (Linux / GTK input).

Good to know

  • AltGr keys assume the US International (AltGr) OS layout: AltGr+Q = ä, S = ß, P = ö, Y = ü, E = é.
  • Macro M6 («) sends its key-up before the key-down, unlike the others. It may not trigger the Unicode prompt; swap those two steps in Vial if « doesn't type.
  • Macro M11 (Super+Shift+N, then types “mail”) is defined but not placed on any key.
  • Five tap dances (! / Alt, % / Ctrl, ^ / RCtrl, ) / Alt, AltGr / Alt) are configured but unused. No combos or key overrides are set; layers 4–9 and the encoders are empty.
